# AUDIT.md — bryancollins.com brand audit (2026-07-01, overnight)

## 1. What the brand currently claims

**Homepage hero:** "I build AI tools and document the process. Creating practical tools
for builders and investor-operators."

The site title still hedges: "Author, Builder & Operator of 7+ Sites." Schema says
"USA Today bestselling author helping writers build sustainable businesses with MBA
frameworks and AI tools." So three identities coexist on one page:

1. **Writing-era author** (books section, BAWT card, "helping writers" schema)
2. **MBA-frameworks educator** (Tools nav, 6 framework pages + 6 calculators, footer columns)
3. **AI builder documenting the process** (hero, Vibe Coding video, projects grid)

The hero already committed to identity #3. The rest of the site hasn't caught up.

## 3. Email reality (Kit API, pulled tonight)

- **Active list: 10,909** (the "16K" figure includes cancelled/cold). 90-day net growth: **zero** (241 in, 241 out).
- Opens stable at 32–36% (~3,700/send) — the remaining list is genuinely engaged.
- **Click behaviour is the brand's real market research:**
  - "How I build" workflow emails: **2.7–3.3% CTR** (135–3,165 clicks)
  - Product-pitch emails (PWS closing sequence): **0.5–0.9% CTR**
  - The audience votes 4–5× for *documented systems* over *courses being sold to them*.
- Purchase history (9 months): $197 launches ≈ 3 units each; $97 ≈ 5 units; $20–47 sells
  steadily in small numbers. **Historical revenue ceiling per campaign ≈ $600.**
- The single largest product-intent event ever recorded: 305 unique clicks on a **$39
  clone-and-run repo of Bryan's own automation scripts** — a live pre-order that **nobody bought** (CORRECTED 2026-07-03: originally mis-read as 'never delivered'; it was delivered as a pre-order and declined at checkout by all 305).

## 4. The gap between site and actual work

Bryan's actual 2026 work (per worklog): AI-orchestrated builds across a portfolio
(Vendors, Tenderwatch, Leinster Insider), Claude Code multi-agent pipelines, Zettelkasten-
driven content ops, deterministic data products. The newsletter already reflects this —
recent sends are Claude Code workflows, AI-built businesses, portfolio operating.

**The site lags the newsletter, not vice versa.** Specific staleness:

- `now.html` — "Last updated: November 2025"; still says "Building 8 MBA framework tools for writers"
- Homepage still sells PWS ($197) as a live product two months after it closed forever
- `products.html` — two "ENROLLMENT CLOSED" waitlists and nothing buyable
- `work-with-me.html` — job-hunting page ("open to Product Leadership or Head of Growth roles") sitting in the top nav of a builder-brand site
- Newsletter subscribe page (Kit-hosted) live typo: "An newsletter aobut AI-built businesses"
- Kit form on homepage says "Join 15,000+ readers" (actual active: 10.9K)
- Repo carries ~20 unlinked design experiments (shadcn-*, simple-black-*, astro-demo-*, youtube-brand-*)

## 5. Honest verdict: what the brand's real remaining asset is

The domain has no meaningful traffic moat (the SEO era is over and Bryan has disowned that
model). The books are back-catalogue. The MBA frameworks are undifferentiated free tools.

**The real remaining asset is 3,700 people who still open Bryan's emails every week and
demonstrably click hardest on one thing: watching him actually build, and wanting the
system he builds with.** Ten years of writing-era trust, already half-bridged to the
builder era by the newsletter itself. The list is flat but not dead — it's engaged and
un-monetised. It also carries one hard data point: a $39 pre-order 305 of them clicked
and none of them bought (CORRECTED 2026-07-03 — not an 'IOU', a failed price test; interest without purchase).

The site's job is not to be a portfolio brochure. It's to be the place where that failed
pre-order gets a fair second test as a finished, look-before-you-pay product — a shopfront for productised versions of the workflows the list already
clicks on.
