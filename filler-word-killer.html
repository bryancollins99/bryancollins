Your text never leaves your browser

This tool runs entirely as JavaScript on this page. When you press Analyse, nothing is sent anywhere: no upload, no API, no server. You can open your browser dev tools, watch the network tab, and confirm zero requests fire while it works. Paste a confidential draft without a second thought.

How it works

It checks your draft against a fixed, human-readable dictionary of filler words, empty intensifiers, hedges, weak verbs, redundant pairs, and over-used -ly adverbs. The rules are deterministic, so the same draft always gets the same read. It is a first-pass editor, not a grammar checker. You still make the calls.
