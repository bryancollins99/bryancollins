Hi Reader

Over a million of young Italians left their country in the past 10 years.

These 25–34-year-olds are saying arrivederci to cities like Rome, Florence and Milan because of low salaries and job opportunities.

Thousands of Irish my age quit the country and boarded planes to countries like Australia and the US because the Irish government bailed out the banks.

A good chunk of these emigrating Irish never came home. A few friends of mine who emigrated only come home at Christmas. The same thing happened here in the 1980s.

Those who stayed called the phenomenon the “Brain drain”. Things were harder then because you couldn’t start an internet business.
