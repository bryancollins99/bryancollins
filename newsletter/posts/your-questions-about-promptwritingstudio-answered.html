Your Questions About PromptWritingStudio… Answered

Earlier this year, I launched PromptWritingStudio. It’s a newsletter course that covers the art of prompt writing. It’ll help you use AI for your content or business.

I was messaging a fractional CMO on LinkedIn the other day, and she asked me a few questions about the course:

Is it every week that I get notified that there’s a new drip in a course-like setting that I have to log into, and then I can view it? In my life, there are weeks when I get really busy, and I need to go in then and catch up on things in bulk later. So, it’s always good to know if it’s use it or lose it.

It’s an email lesson delivered 3 times a week to your inbox. I’ve numbered each lesson so you can find them in your Gmail or Outlook quickly and easily. These bite-sized emails are easy to digest and contain prompts you can use with working examples.

You don’t need to log in to access the content as it's delivered via email.

Is there any kind of support or is this just like, you know, one email and then that’s it? Or is there any kind of, you know, I mean, customer support as well as community feature? I’d really be interested in understanding how it’s structured.

You can email me about the prompts and lessons, and I’ll reply. I’ve also helped a few clients develop custom prompts for their businesses.

Is it for beginner or advanced people?

This course is for business owners, consultants, and creators who already see the potential of AI but feel stuck when it comes to using it effectively in their daily work. You’re using it already, and you want to get more from it.

What will I be able to do after taking the course?

By the end of the course, you’ll be confidently using ChatGPT, Claude and a few other AI tools to do things like:

  • Create authentic content that sounds like you wrote every word
  • Turn 3 hours of repetitive tasks into 20-minute workflows
  • Generate 10+ high-quality content ideas in under 5 minutes
  • Build custom AI systems that support your specific creative process
  • Turn AI into your executive assistant
  • Plan and create a month’s worth of content for social media
