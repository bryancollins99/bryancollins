Resending: I want to connect with other writers

Hi Reader,

I was talking to a new writer recently.

She told me,

“I want to connect with other writers, creators, and thinkers. I haven’t found a welcoming community where I can meet these people—especially beginners.”

Enter a writing community.

In theory… a good one can save you time in several ways.

You can…

Ask questions about the craft.

Connect with other writers.

Get accountability for key writing projects.

Share wins.

The problem?

Many public writing communities are stuffed with trolls and AI bots (ahem, Reddit!).

It’s hard to ask questions in public communities without getting shouted or voted down.

Many private communities are pricey and hard to get into.

No wonder beginners find it hard to connect with other writers.

That’s one of the reasons why I’m launching the Become a Writer Today community.

I’m planning on holding a monthly AMA (ask me anything) call for community members.

I’ll post a weekly AMA thread so you can get feedback on your work and start getting paid to write.

I’ve also prepared a series of workbooks, templates, and swipe files you can use immediately.

These work for articles, online writing, and even books.

If you get stuck, I’ll supplement them with bite-sized lessons.

The Become a Writer Today community costs $19.95 per month, but you can cancel anytime.

If enough people join this week, I’ll open the doors later this month.

As a bonus, I’ll also give you the Become a Writer Today best-selling series of writing books.
