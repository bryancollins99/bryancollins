How I Turned My Productivity System into a 4-Figure Payday

A few months ago, I geeked on Notion.

I learn how things work by using them.

So, I built a custom Notion dashboard for myself.

I use it to track my writing, online projects, content, and stats.

A former geek, I’m big into self-quantification.

I recorded a tutorial about how my Notion system works.

And I created a sales page using Caard.

Total time spent?

3-4 hours.

I didn’t plan a big launch or promotional campaign.

I wrote a few emails about The Digital Writer’s Command Center and how I use it.

Then, I sent the emails to my newsletter with a link to the sales page.

To date, The Digital Writer’s Command Center has generated over four figures in sales.

Customers said they liked the price point.

That’s a nice amount of revenue for a tool I use inside my business.

Granted, I know how to write emails and sales pages, but my technique isn’t proprietary.

You can easily follow my process.

I was coaching a STEM client recently.

A former engineer, she showed me some templates and experiments she created.

She can put these together fast because she has years of experience in her niche.

Her resources are packed with VALUE.

And her audience?

Well, they’d happily pay for this type of content.

I know because I researched her niche on ETSY and found STEM templates and experiments of much lower quality selling by the dozen.

All my client has to do is productize.

The lesson here?

You can mine nuggets of gold from your creative process.

To do it…

Consider content formats you enjoy working on.

A good chunk of your audience finds this type of creative work challenging.

They’d love to hear about how you do it.

So don’t disappoint.

You could turn your content, system, or process into a paid product.

It could be a template, a mini-course, a workshop, or a swipe file.

You see…

If you can solve a problem for your audience, they’ll happily pay for it. And with that income, you can invest in creating even better products.
