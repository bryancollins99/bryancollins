What is PromptWritingStudio?

Earlier this year, I created an email course about writing with AI called PromptWritingStudio.

I tested it with a small subset of my email list as a beta program. Customers got two proven AI prompts they can use with ChatGPT, delivered to their inbox twice weekly.

I launched the newsletter course using a simple Google Doc and Kit’s checkout cart. The course was a surprising success.

What began as a quick test with a handful of subscribers turned into one of my most profitable offers.

So I invested more time and energy into improving the course and deliverables.

Now, PromptWritingStudio is a much more involved course. It contains dozens of practical lessons, video tutorials, and workflows for creators and business owners. It’ll help you use AI to improve content quality while spending less time on the content hamster wheel. I cover how to use AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.

I’ve also moved away from using a Google Doc and am building an entire site dedicated to PromptWritingStudio. The website contains examples from the course and offers a simplified purchase experience for customers.

I did this because my process involves validating simple ideas with Google Docs before investing more time into turning them into polished products.

Fun fact: I used an AI coding tool (Windsurf) to build this website.

PromptWritingStudio isn’t just another AI course—it’s a practical system that delivers ready-to-use AI prompts directly to your inbox, allowing you to implement professional-grade AI workflows immediately without the learning curve.

It now contains three tiers:

Basic: $25/month - the most affordable version. You get two email lessons weekly with proven AI prompts and workflows.

Pro tier: All of the above plus access to monthly live workshops running 60–90 minutes where I show what’s working with the latest AI tools. Just last week, I ran a popular workshop called “The AI Humanizer.”

Elite tier: Access to all private training including past and future workshops, plus one-to-one support implementing AI in your business. This costs $97 per month. I’ll cap the number of people who can buy this tier as 1:1 access is time intensive.

PromptWritingStudio differs from my daily newsletter, which you’re reading 🙂

It’s an email course with lessons delivered straight to your inbox that you can access anywhere. The workshops and videos from the Pro Tier are hosted inside Teachable, the online learning platform I’ve used for years.

I considered moving away from Teachable, but I decided to stick with it after feedback from past students.

If you have questions about joining PromptWritingStudio or your membership, feel free to reply. The course comes with a 14-day money back guarantee.

If you’re currently a member and would like to upgrade, check out the website and reply back - I’ll change your subscription.

I’ll tell you more about what I followed this approach to offer creation later this week.
