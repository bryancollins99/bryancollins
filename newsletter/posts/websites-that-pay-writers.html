9 websites that pay writers

Hi Reader

I get lots of emails from readers and newer freelancers looking for paying writing work.

Alas, I’m not hiring or offering paying writing work.

I’d much prefer you spend time working on your business instead of someone else’s. That starts with building an asset you own, like an email list.

It ends with creating a high-ticker offer like coaching and consulting.

Building your content business is profitable, rewarding, and easy to do.

That’s how I spend my days.

I have no illusions, though. Building a content business takes time, trial, and error. Don’t let any guru tell you otherwise. And many people don’t have enough cash to focus on building.
