How I turned 1,000+ notes into content gold with Claude

Claude just became my secret weapon for content creation. I fed it my entire library of notes—thousands of markdown files—and built four custom scripts that now run my content planning on autopilot.

Here's what these scripts actually do:

Deep Pattern Analyzer: Runs weekly to audit how I spent my time, spots contradictions in my work, identifies cross-domain patterns, and even suggests reflection questions for journaling. It's like having an AI accountability coach that actually reads my notes.

Content Pipeline Generator: Scans my recent notes and work log to suggest LinkedIn posts, newsletter topics, and YouTube videos. No more staring at a blank screen wondering what to create next.

The other two scripts handle the boring stuff—exporting Apple Notes, fixing broken links, and finding duplicates across thousands of files.

What makes this powerful isn't the AI—it's the system. I'm not asking Claude to write my content. I'm using it to surface patterns and ideas I'd never spot manually across years of accumulated knowledge.

The video shows exactly how I set this up, including the actual code and sample outputs from each script. If you've got a growing collection of notes but struggle to turn them into consistent content, this approach could change how you work.
