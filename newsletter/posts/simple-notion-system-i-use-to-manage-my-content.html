The simple Notion system I use to manage my content

Your existing content is like an untapped gold mine - most creators keep digging new holes instead of mining what they already have.

I’ve spent years writing online,

It can easily become a full-time, low-paying job if you keep digging.

One method for getting around this problem is building a content library of your greatest hits. It’s easier to repurpose content if you have a library that you can draw from.

Another method is tracking a few key weekly stats across each channel so you can see what’s working.

To do this for my business, I built a Notion system. I use it to plan, publish, and track my content via a simple but effective Noton dashboard. Because of this system, I can create a week’s worth of content in about 60 minutes.
