I built niche websites for years

Hi Reader,

I quit building niche websites.

I built them for years.

I got millions of page views.

And I earned multiple six figures from this business model.

I recommended this business model to other writers.

I was even offered 6 figures for one site.

And 7 figures for another.

But it all crashed and burned.

In 2024, I quit the niche website game.

Building niche websites WAS a great model for writers and bloggers.

Not anymore.

  1. Competition is fierce
  2. AI is lowering the barrier to entry
  3. Google hates content websites
  4. Search engines are using your content for free
  5. Social media platforms are using AI too

I’ve abandoned most of my content websites.

I picked a different business model.

So what am I doing now?

I’m doubling down on my newsletter.

I’m doubling down on coaching, copywriting and client work.

I’m writing what AI can’t.

Watch my video above for examples of how you can rework your writing business.

Write on,

Bryan Collins
