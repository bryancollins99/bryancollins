All my red hot writing secrets…

Hi Reader,

Profitable writers don’t earn money directly from one book.

The pro-fiction authors I met?

They have a rich back catalog that helps them pay their bills.

And they regularly bundle their most popular books and sell them as a series.

And non-fiction authors?

Their book(s) form part of an ecosystem of products.

That book could land them a public speaking gig, consulting, or help them sell a course.

Successful authors of all genres regularly update their old products, services, and pricing to reflect their audience’s needs.

I’ve interviewed dozens of best-selling New York Times and USA Today authors who follow this approach.

I did something similar, too.

In fact:

I wrote a few books about writing and creativity.

And I created a dozen different writing courses over the past few years.

I bundled some of my writing books and courses a while ago.

I called it the Successful Writer’s Club.

The Club contains a series of mini-courses and books.

I teach topics like:

  • Book writing

  • Blogging for writers

That’s a lot to teach inside one offer.
