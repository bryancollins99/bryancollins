How I Repurpose One Email into 10+ Pieces of Content

Hi Reader,

I'm going to give you a simple strategy for repurposing your content.

And you can do it with AI.

This strategy saves me hours of time every week.

Not only that but it gives me plenty of opportunities to take content that I've written and improve it.

It's something that I would have paid someone to do a few years ago.

But now it's possible with AI because I've spent a couple of hours training it to write and work like I do.

Here's how my strategy works.

Once per day, I write and send a daily email to subscribers of my email list.

I can write about what's happening in my business.

I can write about creating content online.

I can also write about how I'm helping clients.

I write a 400 - 500 word email with a clear offer.

However, repurposing that content for social media, while a smart strategy, can turn into a full-time job.

Who has time to create dozens of Tweets, Facebook posts, LinkedIn posts, Pinterest pins and so on?

Not me.

That was until I built my own bot with AI.

My emails are what you call medium-form content.

I spend about 30 minutes writing one of these emails.

It has a good headline, a story that relates to my business or whatever I'm working on and then some takeaways or insights for subscribers.

Finally it has a clear offer which helps me bring in income and grow my business.

Now, rather than creating loads of content for social media based on the email to grow my list of subscribers, I simply repurpose this email.
