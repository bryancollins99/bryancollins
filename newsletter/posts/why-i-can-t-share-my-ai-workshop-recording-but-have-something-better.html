Why I can't share my AI workshop recording (but have something better)

Yesterday, I held a live workshop for creators who want to use AI without losing their voice.

On The Art of Prompt Writing workshop, I showcased how to set up and train ChatGPT and Claude on writing samples for different creative and business projects.

I also covered a variety of use cases, including content repurposing and writing daily Substack notes. I also geeked out about ChatGPT voice mode, which I've hotwired to the Quick Action button on my iPhone.

A few subscribers emailed me last night asking if they could get a recording or watch the workshop after the event.

First the bad news:

As promised on the sales page, I ran the workshop live for ticket holders only. So, I can't send a recording to those who didn't pick up a ticket beforehand, as I’d be breaking my word. We’re all about words around these parts 🙂

Now the good news:

I'm building a private newsletter for creators using AI.

It’s called PromptWritingStudio.

If you want to outsource your thinking to AI completely, this newsletter won't serve you. It's for creators who want to use AI while maintaining their creative voice. Several times a week, I share prompts to help you use tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, and other underground AI tools quickly and easily.

PromptWritingStudio isn't just a collection of prompts, though - it's a complete system for training AI to amplify your creativity and ROI.

The newsletter is in beta with several dozen clients. It only costs $20. I'll close the beta this weekend, so lock in this pricing while you can.

If you’ve questions about joining PromptWritingStudio or what’s inside, reply and ask me.
