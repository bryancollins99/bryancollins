$344 for a flat white?

Hi Reader,

A Scottish dairy farmer is selling the UK’s most expensive cup of coffee.

You can buy a flat white from him for a whopping $344. That’s over 80 times the price of a regular cup of coffee in the UK.

Why would anyone spend this much on their morning beverage? Well, you’re not just investing in a morning pick-me-up.

Bruce Cunningham is selling shares in a crowdfunding Mossgiel Organic Dairy campaign to produce more milk. He wants to expand his operation out of Scotland to sell organic coffee in London.

Perhaps a latte would have been better for Bruce’s marketing stunt? It’s a larger drink with more milk. But let’s face it: coffee snobs prefer a good flat white over, even if it doesn’t last as long.
