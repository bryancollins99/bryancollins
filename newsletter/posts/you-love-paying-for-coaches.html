"You love paying for coaches"

Hi Reader,

My wife says I spend too much on coaching.

It's a running joke in our family.

But she has a point.

I've spent thousands on coaches over the years.

I'm in the middle of growing a YouTube channel, so I hired a coach to help me figure out what to do.

Last Winter...

I hired a marathon coach who helped me get a personal best in a Spring race.

A few years ago, I hired a blogging coach who helped me figure out how to write online.

I even flew over to Austin, Texas, to meet him.

(That was after his advice helped me earn money from blogging).

And before that...

I hired a creative writing coach who helped me learn how to write non-fiction.

I could go on about some of the other coaches I've worked with, but here's the thing...

If you want to learn how to achieve something faster, a coach can help you get there.

Ideally, find a coach three or four steps ahead of you.

These types of coaches are affordable and can save you a lot of head-scratching.

Sure you can take a course...

Or figure it out yourself...

But it's almost faster and less painful to ask someone who's doing what you want to do.

Like earning a living from blogging.

Or writing online.

Or building a writing business.

Write on,

Bryan Collins
