Pop quiz time: How good are you at writing prompts for AI?

How good are you at writing prompts for AI?

Anyone can bang in a request to ChatGPT or Claude and get a reasonably valuable output.

I do that when I’m in a rush, and it’s kind of like using AI to drive a screw into a wall.

Advanced prompting transforms AI into a precision Swiss Army knife. A well-crafted prompt is like selecting the right tool.

I get much better outputs when I spend five or ten minutes writing an effective prompt.

Essentially, I assign AI a specific role and clearly define what I need. I also write up a desired output and provide training data.

It’s time well spent, as I can re-use the prompt or configure a custom Claude or ChatGPT project with it.

Writing effective prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini has a few other nuances.

If you’d like to see what those are, I created a simple prompt. It’ll test your prompt writing skills (from beginner to expert) and give you a few actionable tips.
