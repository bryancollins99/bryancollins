The secret system turns my content into cash

Tomorrow morning, I’ll go for a three or four hour bike ride around Glendalough. That’s a hilly national park in Wicklow, Ireland. I’m training for a triathlon this Summer, and my coach says the more time I spend on long, hilly bike rides, the better.

He gives me a training plan to follow every week. When I’ve questions about a swim, run or bike session, I shoot him a quick WhatsApp message.

I could probably figure out the answers myself via a determined Google search… or hours wasted on Reddit. But having someone I can message with questions cuts down second-guessing. Instead of gazing into my phone, I can get on the bike and log the session. Sometimes, I also hire a coach to avoid second guessing myself.

You see…
