My super simple system for attracting LinkedIn Leads

You don’t need 100k followers to land premium clients. You need the right system.

Today’s email is only relevant if you’re…

  1. Using LinkedIn for your business or brand
  2. Looking for more organic leads from LinkedIn

I’ve refined a neat system that reaches my ideal customers and clients on LinkedIn. My system doesn’t involve standard advice you’ll read, like trying to go viral or commenting on dozens of posts by big guru-types.

I don’t have to spend hours daily glued to my feed. It works on autopilot and helps me land a few high-quality leads every week. I like writing on LinkedIn, but this system works even if you don’t post regularly.

I get about 30-40 leads per month from this system. If that doesn’t sound like much, remember these leads aren’t your typical email subscribers. They’re founders, CEOs, marketing directors, and so on. I’m not trying to sell them information products. They’ve money to invest in products and services that grow their businesses, including a content strategy package I offer with a marketing agency.

If you’re interested in learning more about how this system works, reply to this email and tell me about your business. I can help you set it up.
