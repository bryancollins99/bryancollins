The MBA framework that’s actually useful

Last week, I cracked open my MBA strategy textbook after a hiatus.

Some MBA frameworks are the corporate equivalent of watching paint dry.

But I stumbled across one that’s actually useful: the McKinsey 7-S Framework.

And no, you don’t need an MBA or a six-figure consulting gig to use it.

This week, I applied it to my local newsletter project. The one about events in Leinster that I wrote to you about earlier this month.

The framework helped me spot gaps I’d been ignoring—like the fact I had no real systems in place and my “strategy” was basically “wing it and see what happens.”

Not exactly a recipe for scale.

The McKinsey 7-S Framework breaks your business into seven elements. Three hard elements (Strategy, Structure, Systems).

And four soft ones (Skills, Staff, Style, Shared Values).
