[Tutorial] How I create landing pages in 10 minutes

Hi Reader,

Designing a landing page trips many people up.

I was on a call with a marketer who presented a landing page that looked like….a Gingerbread house!

She explained to Zoom attendees, “Visitors can click on the windows on my house. We'll send them a PDF if they get the right one.”

The team never signed off on her landing page.

You see…

A good landing page either sells or converts.

It’s not a puzzle.

And it’s not a work of art.

Send traffic to your landing page from social media or paid ads.

Give away a freebie, like a template, checklist, or report, in exchange for an email address.

And grow your email list.

Simple, right?

And yet, many creators and even marketers get hung up on creating the PERFECT landing page.

They obsess over fancy designs and interactive elements, I focus on the only metric that matters: conversion rates.

I’ve tried dozens of landing page tools. I use Leadpages because it’s fast, and I can sort templates by conversion rates. I don’t worry about perfection, only optins!
