Little writing

Hi Reader,

I stumbled across the concept of little writing a few years ago.

No, I don’t mean ornate, cursive prose.

Write a little every day, instead of a lot somedays.

You see…

Why sweat trying to write a few thousand words?

Why force yourself to sit in the chair for hours?

Instead, write something short when you think of it.

Record an exciting idea.

A single sentence.

A few bullet points.

An entire paragraph.

Bang it out on your phone.

Build a little library of little notes and ideas.

And add to it every day.

Then…

Read through your notes when you’ve a little more time.

Return to a few older ideas.

Add to them.

And slowly…

Your little ideas will grow.

They’ll flourish.

And you’ll have something you can publish.

If you need help with little writing, I created a little course that can help.

Write on,
Bryan Collins
