My $10 Sunday habit that's worth $10,000

Every Sunday morning, I spend exactly $10.

My wife thinks I'm mad. The kids roll their eyes.

But by December, this tiny habit will be worth 1000x that investment.

Here's what I buy: domain names.

Last Sunday: cryptonewsflash.com

Week before: buildmyhomegym.com

Before that: MyTriathlonCalculator.com (which I actually built)

Most of these domains sit dormant.

A few become fun side projects. One or two turn into real businesses.

I sold one of my side projects for multi-5 figures early this year.

The magic isn't in collecting random domains—it's in training my brain to spot opportunities everywhere.

Each purchase forces me to think: "What problem could this solve? Who would pay for this solution?"

It's like doing bicep curls for my entrepreneurial muscle.

That Sunday triathlon calculator domain?

I turned it into a full web app using AI in 2 hours. Cost me $10 to buy, $20 for the AI tool subscription.

A developer quoted me $2,000 for the same work.

Will it turn into a business?

Too soon to say, but I only need one or two winners.

Most overpriced business gurus — and I've paid for my fair share —tell you to focus.

Pick ONE thing. Go deep.

But focus without options is just tunnel vision. These tiny bets give me creator leverage.

When an AI model launches or a new trend emerges, I've already secured some digital real estate.

What $10-20 experiment could you run next week?
