Peak inside my AI-powered toolstack

A subscriber asked what tools I use to run my newsletter business.

I’m a reformed tool collector.

I’ve seen creators spend more time researching productivity apps than actually creating. They’ll debate Notion vs. Obsidian for weeks while their email list sits at 12 subscribers.

I did the same thing!

My approach these days? Find a few tools that work, stick with them, and get back to creating and growing your list!

Occasionally, I’ll try a new AI tool but only after I’ve created some content or shipped an offer for the day (more on that in a moment).

Here’s my current tool stack:

Writing
The Archive - $19.99
Apple Notes (free) - Quick capture for ideas on the go
Grammarly - $29.99 (use my 20% discount here)

I use these every day

AI
Claude ($20/month) - My creative partner for ideation and content repurposing
ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) - Executive assistant and business coach
Perplexity ($20/month) - Google killer in the making

Email Marketing
Kit (email marketing) - Around $79/month for my list size
Substack (free) - Growing my audience with daily notes
Ghost (free) - I use this for a local newsletter I run

Video & Design
Camtasia (one-time) - Recording and editing YouTube videos
Canva Pro ($15/month) - Quick graphics when needed

Courses
Teachable (varies) - I’ve tried a few other online platforms and wasted dozens of hours procrastinating instead of creating. I keep coming back to Teachable.

Business Operations
Stripe (2.9% fees) - Payment processing
Google Workspace ($7/month) - Email and file storage

Websites
WordPress - free, excluding hosting

Vibe Coding
Cursor AI - $20 per month
Netlify + GitHub - free

You don’t need these to run a newsletter business! I only include them here as I’m teaching myself to vibe code on the side of my business.

I’d rather spend 20 minutes writing this email than 2 hours setting up the “perfect” system.

What matters isn’t having the shiniest tools—it’s shipping consistently. Take those vibe coding tools I mentioned. I’ll only experiment with shiny tools like this after I’ve worked on my newsletter business for the day i.e., written an email, made an offer, tweaked an ad.
