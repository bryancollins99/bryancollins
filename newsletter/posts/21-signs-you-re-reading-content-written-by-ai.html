21 signs you’re reading content written by AI.

Hey AI! Get your fingerprints off my content...

Here are 21 signs you’re reading content written by a bot.

  1. You spot lots of emojis 😂😍😔
  2. Or dashes –
  3. The content is full of filler phrases like “It’s important to note” and “Before we begin…..”
  4. It repeats the same idea, over and over, in different ways. Again and again.
  5. The prose reads as overpolished and robotic because that’s what gets a 100% score in a grammar checker.
  6. The passive voice is overused.
  7. AI stacks the adjectives and adverbs efficiently but rather ineffectively on top of each other.
  8. It overexplains simple topics, such as why generic AI content can be boring to read.
  9. The content is stuffed with SEO keywords like “Best AI writing tips”, and “tips for writing AI content,” and “how to write with AI”
  10. It hedges like a lawyer who might get into trouble for saying ,maybe, the wrong thing
  11. The content addresses a simple topic in waaaay too much detail.
  12. The real-world examples don’t quite fit the context. You’re reading about “How to write better AI prompts,” and suddenly the writer says something like, “Just like planting tomatoes in winter, timing matters.”
  13. It states the obvious. “AI wrote this.”
  14. It’s maddeningly positive: “I encourage you to fact-check what AI generates.”
  15. It plops in generic qualifiers like “further research needed”
  16. You spot vague call-to-actions like “take action now”
  17. The content doesn’t deliver on the intro. “This prompt writing strategy will change your business!”
  18. It’s full of weird analogies and metaphors telling you things like “Creativity flows like a broken garden hose”
  19. 9/10 of the stats lack references
  20. The arguments are oddly balanced. “On the one hand…” and “On the other hand…”
  21. It wraps up by telling you “In conclusion…”
