Steal my "Template Pattern" for AI workouts

I’m in the middle of a training block for a 50k race.

My coach is great…

But sometimes I need to generate a specific workout on the fly when life gets in the way.

I used to ask ChatGPT: “Give me a conditioning workout.”

And it would spit out a wall of text.

Sometimes it was a CrossFit WOD.

Sometimes it was a bodybuilding split.

It was messy, not useful.

So, I started using the Template Pattern.

It sounds fancy, but it’s just a way of putting a straitjacket on the AI.

I tell the AI exactly what format I want the output in, using brackets.

Here is the prompt I use for my gym sessions:

"When I ask for a workout, generate it using the following template. Replace the bracketed text with specific details:
Workout Name: [Name]Type: [Strength/Run/Intervals]Reps/Distance: [Range or Distance]Sets: [Number]Intensity: [RPE or Heart Rate Zone]Rest: [Time between sets]Duration: [Total time]Difficulty: [Scale 1-5]"

Now, AI gives me exactly what I need, instantly.

No waffle or “Here is a workout I think you will like.”

Just the data.

(Bonus points if you upload past workouts as training data).
