Stop manually organizing your notes (AI does it faster)

You have hundreds of notes scattered across different topics, but finding the right one when you need it feels impossible. Bryan discovered a game-changing solution using Claude Code that transforms chaos into organized "maps of contents."

Here's what the video covers:

The Map of Contents Method: Instead of manually reviewing notes one by one (which takes hours), you create single documents that collate all notes on specific topics. Think of it as your personal knowledge GPS.

AI-Powered Organization: Bryan shows exactly how he uses Claude Code to automatically generate these maps by pointing it at his note library. What used to take hours of manual work now happens in minutes for about $2 worth of tokens.

Real Examples in Action: See his actual maps for MBA strategic frameworks, AI tools, and endurance training - complete with hyperlinked navigation between related notes and automatic categorization.

The breakthrough moment? Bryan went from procrastinating on note organization (because it felt like busy work) to having multiple comprehensive maps that he actually uses and updates regularly.

This isn't just about productivity - it's about making your accumulated knowledge truly accessible when you need it most.
