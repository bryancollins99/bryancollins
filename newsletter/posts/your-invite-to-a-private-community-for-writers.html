Your invite to a private community for writers

Hi Reader,

Earlier this year, I started working directly with writers who want to go pro.

I created Pro Writers Only.

It's a 1:1 coaching program.

I help clients earn an extra $3-5k a month from their writing businesses.

This premium program isn't for everyone.

Also, I only accept 10 clients.

And it's closed until later this Summer.

However, some 20,000 writers read this newsletter.

Readers regularly email me saying they still need support.

They want to connect with other writers, share their work, and get feedback.

I craved all these things too when I started my writing business.

So, I'm considering launching the Become a Writer Today community.

It's an affordable option for writers who want hands-on support while growing their network… but don’t need hands-on coaching..

You can post your content, and I'll give you feedback.

I'll also provide resources for getting paid to write, and for building your online profile.

If enough people sign up to the waitlist, I'll open the doors.

Otherwise, I'll refund you the cost of joining :)

It costs $19.95 per month.
