My new favorite teacher is a robot 🤖

My new favourite teacher is a robot.

Earlier this month, I decided to geek out on a new skill: Python for Finance.

I’ve traded options on and off for a while now.

I even enjoy the thrill of the “Wheel Strategy” (a strategy for generating income from stocks).

But Python?

That's more challenging.

A few years ago, I would have quit.

Or I would have spent hours falling down a YouTube rabbit hole, trying to figure out what a "float" is.

Enter Google Guided Learning.

It’s a new feature inside Gemini.

Basically, "Gems" are Google's answer to custom GPTs.

So, I created a custom Gem for learning Python for finance.

When I get stuck on a lesson—which happens every 10 minutes—I plug in the transcript.

Sometimes, I take a screenshot of the code I’ve butchered and upload it.

Then, I ask my Gem: "Explain this to me like I'm five."

And it gives me a step-by-step breakdown.

It doesn’t judge me for asking basic questions.

It doesn’t get tired.

And it connects the dots between the code and the financial concepts I already know.

I also spun up a second Gem specifically for options trading technical analysis. It walked me through a few concepts regarding volatility that I’d completely overlooked.

AI isn't just for writing emails or generating images.

It’s the ultimate accelerator for learning.
